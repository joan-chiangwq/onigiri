Meet Onigiri 🍙

Perfect for

  • Developers running long builds, tests or downloads
  • Creatives waiting for exports and renders
  • Presenters who can’t have their screen dim mid-talk
  • Anyone who just wants their Mac to stay awake without fiddling aimlessly with System Settings

One-click to caffeinate, one-click to snooze.

Onigiri is the smallest possible macOS utility tool for keeping a Mac awake. It lives quietly in the menu bar as a tiny rice ball. When you need your Mac to stay awake, feed it a coffee. When you’re done, let it nap.

Installation via

Homebrew

  1. Open Terminal.
  2. Add the Onigiri catalog. Paste this into your Terminal:
    brew tap joan-chiangwq/onigiri https://github.com/joan-chiangwq/onigiri
  3. Trust the catalog. Homebrew asks you to confirm catalogs from outside Homebrew. Paste this:
    brew trust joan-chiangwq/onigiri
  4. Install Onigiri. Paste this into your Terminal:
    brew install --cask onigiri
  5. Open Onigiri. Find it in your Applications folder.

Direct Release

  1. Download the installer with the button below.
  2. Open the downloaded file (Onigiri.dmg) from your Downloads folder.
  3. Drag the Onigiri icon onto the Applications folder in the window that opens.
  4. Open Onigiri from your Applications folder. If your Mac asks "Are you sure you want to open it?", click Open. Onigiri is signed and checked by Apple. This is just a first-time prompt.
Download 🍙

Look up! A 🍙 appears in your menu bar, at the top right of your screen. Click it and flip the switch to keep your Mac awake.

Don’t see it in your Dock? That’s normal. Onigiri lives only in your menu bar.

FAQ

Why did you build this?

I kept forgetting to keep my Mac awake during downloads, presentations, and long-running tasks. Every app I found was full of switches, menus, and settings. So I built one that lives quietly in the menu bar as a tiny rice ball. Click once when you need it. Click again when you don't.

Does it use lots of battery?

No. Onigiri itself uses almost no power. Keeping your screen on does use some battery, so let it nap when you’re done.

Does it collect data?

No, we do not collect any data at all. Onigiri doesn’t change any of your settings. It only asks macOS to stay awake while it’s on. Once a day it checks for updates, which sends no personal information.
